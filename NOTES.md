# Notes

This system uses only Python's standard library and runs fully offline. No API keys, paid services, network calls or language models are required. submission/solution.py exposes build(); the implementation lives in small modules under submission/ks/. Ingest preserves complete snapshots and indexes structured markdown rows, paragraphs and compact sections. The solution does not load evaluation files.

Current means the latest supplied crawl. Historical questions select the latest crawl on or before the requested date, with the first crawl used for earlier dates. Central rate sheets outrank embedded product tables and archived content. Queries resolve product family, term, model-year range, dollar tier, business scope, jumbo status and metric. Year parsing supports shortened and reversed ranges and older-than tiers. Dollar parsing supports optional repeated currency symbols, decimals and open upper bounds. Scoped questions select one authoritative row deterministically, so competing tables do not produce two values for one fact. Illustrative payment examples are excluded from rate fallback evidence.

Prose retrieval combines binary-term BM25, generic vocabulary expansion, phrase coverage, named entities, page authority and answer-shape features. Newsroom publication dates help distinguish recent statements from historical articles. URL answers and extra product citations use links extracted from the selected snapshot, without hard-coded target URLs. Unknown table terms and unknown branch entities abstain without introducing digits. A general exact-match non-rate fee fallback preserves the last observed citation when a fee page disappears and labels that limitation. General hours answers use observed schedules and explain that schedules vary by branch.

Conflict extraction compares matching product, term, balance and metric identities across snapshots and mirrored tables. It normalizes presentation differences and rolling auto age-band labels, then resolves to the latest authoritative observation. Distinct tiers and APR versus APY remain separate. The current corpus yields 62 conflicts, but no full-site gold is supplied to establish their precision. Every source includes a URL, snapshot identifier and evidence quote.

The latest offline public QA and final scores are 0.9070, with zero stale-trap hits and p95 answer time of 0.53 seconds. All 35 extra questions pass, including new vehicle synonyms, older model years, reversed model-year bands, recreational amount tiers, home equity lines, additional deposit products and historical selection. The harness self-test scores 1.0000, including conflict F1 and resolution accuracy of 1.0000. Five unit tests pass. Full-site ingest takes about nine seconds. Reports are under results/. Public and extra scores have no conflict pillar, and practice scores do not predict hidden-set performance.

Remaining limitations include lexical ambiguity, imperfect prose extraction, nonuniform publication dates and potentially outdated evidence on removed fee pages. The public volunteer expectation uses an older reporting-year count than the newer article retrieved by the system; no practice-specific exception is added. The corpus cannot establish changes after its last crawl.
